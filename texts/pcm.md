# Di Nọ̀t Wind an di Sun

**Naijá**

Di Nọ̀t Wind an di Sun dey argue about which one among dem strong pass. Just den, dem see one traveller wey dey come for road, wrap imsef with cloak. Dem agree say anybody wey fit make di traveller comot im cloak, na im strong pass. Di Nọ̀t Wind begin blow with all im power. But as e dey blow harder, na so di traveller dey hold di cloak tighter round im body. At last, di Nọ̀t Wind give up. Den di Sun shine warm light on di traveller, and di traveller comot im cloak at once. So di Nọ̀t Wind gree say di Sun strong pass am.
