# Henndu Fuɗnaange e Naange

**Fulfulde**

Henndu Fuɗnaange e Naange ina kaɓa hol e maɓɓe ɓurɗo semmbe. Tuma ɗon ɓe njiyii yahoowo ina ara e laawol, ina ɓoornii wutte. Ɓe nanondiri wonde kala baawɗo waɗde yahoowo oo itti wutte mum, ko kañum ɓurɗo semmbe. Henndu Fuɗnaange fuɗɗii ɓuuɓde e semmbe mum fof. Kono ko ɓurɗe ɓuuɓde, ko noon yahoowo oo ɓeydirnoo ɓoornaade wutte mum tiiɗɗe. Haa wattude, Henndu Fuɗnaange woppii. Ndeen Naange yaynii e nguleeki dow yahoowo oo, o itti wutte mum ɗoon e ɗoon. Kanko ɗon Henndu Fuɗnaange waɗi ko jaɓa wonde Naange ɓuri mo semmbe.
